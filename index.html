First release

Modular Medieval Village Kit

Build your own medieval town – piece by piece or with ready-made houses. All pieces share one metric grid, so walls, roofs, floors and props snap together cleanly.

  • 159modular pieces
  • 20house prefabs
  • 16URP materials
  • 41textures, 2048²
  • URPUnity 6.5 or newer

Coming soon on the Unity Asset Store.

159 modular pieces

  • 68 walls, 1–6 m wide: ground floor (3 m) and upper floor (2.5 m), with windows, doors, corners and gables
  • 17 roof parts (4–10 m) with dormer, gable beams and bargeboards
  • 22 frames and pergolas
  • 43 props: balconies, stairs, ladders, railings, floors, window canopies and shutters, rope bridges
  • 6 small buildings and 3 door leaves

Ready to use

  • 20 complete house prefabs, built as nested prefabs
  • Box and mesh colliders, real-world scale (1 unit = 1 m)
  • Seamless PBR textures: 3 m per texture repeat
  • 2 demo scenes and a PDF guide with module catalog
  • Art assets only – no scripts

About

Lix300 is a one-person studio from Switzerland. I model modular 3D environments in Blender and prepare them for Unity, so you can spend your time on level design instead of fixing assets.

Textures are based on CC0 images from Poly Haven.
